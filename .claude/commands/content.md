# Production Pup: Content

Run the content-quality and AI-style authenticity audit.

Read `SKILL.md` first, then read `references/ai-content-authenticity.md` and apply it where relevant.

## Command

`/content`

Audit and, when requested, fix:

- generic or repetitive AI-style phrasing
- stock openers and formulaic framing
- unnecessary rhetorical questions
- cliche framing devices
- excessive emoji use in product/content copy
- over-explaining obvious points
- content with little product-specific substance
- missing real examples, evidence, or useful details
- inconsistent terminology or brand voice
- unsupported claims
- filler content, placeholder copy, and generic marketing language

Treat these as style signals, not proof that AI wrote the content. Do not deliberately introduce typos or awkward grammar to make writing appear human.

Never invent customer stories, credentials, personal experience, metrics, research, reviews, or product capabilities to make copy feel authentic.

## Verification

Compare important copy against the actual product, routes, features, data, and supplied evidence. Prefer specific, useful writing that reflects the real product and audience.
