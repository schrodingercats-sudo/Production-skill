# Trust, Conversion & Local SEO Reference

Use this reference for websites where the goal is to turn visitors into real inquiries, bookings, customers, or local leads without fabricating trust signals.

The checks below are derived from the supplied video references. They complement, rather than replace, the core SEO, UX, performance, and content sections in `SKILL.md`.

## Website trust and conversion checks

Audit for:

- unique page titles
- prominent, clear CTAs
- short forms that ask only for information needed at that stage
- a clear pricing section when pricing is relevant to the buying decision
- custom page-specific meta descriptions
- a real, tested booking flow when the site offers booking
- a descriptive homepage headline that quickly explains what the business/product does
- a useful sticky mobile contact action when direct contact is a primary conversion path
- an FAQ section containing genuinely useful questions and answers where appropriate
- relevant internal links between related pages
- a configured and useful Google Search Console setup when the project owner controls it
- removal of fake reviews and testimonials
- dedicated service/product pages when separate offerings need separate search or conversion contexts
- real case studies when the business claims case-study evidence
- descriptive alt text for meaningful images
- a clear response-time promise only when the business can actually honor it
- a booking confirmation page/state after successful booking
- visible, usable contact details
- compressed and appropriately sized images

Do not fabricate reviews, case studies, response-time promises, pricing, or business details just to satisfy a checklist.

## Local SEO checks

For businesses serving a specific location, audit for:

1. Google Search Console setup.
2. Sitemap submitted where applicable.
3. Google Analytics or another legitimate analytics system where the owner has chosen to use analytics.
4. Google Business Profile where applicable and controlled by the business.
5. Main search keyword represented naturally in the relevant page title when appropriate.
6. City/location included where it genuinely describes the service area.
7. Useful meta descriptions.
8. Relevant internal links.
9. Compressed images and sensible image delivery.
10. Legitimate backlinks or a realistic plan to earn them, rather than spammy link schemes.

These are discoverability checks, not guarantees of rankings. Never stuff keywords, invent a location, or create fake backlinks.

## Verification

Test real user paths where possible:

- click every primary CTA
- submit booking/contact flows in a safe test environment
- verify confirmation and failure states
- verify contact links on mobile
- inspect page titles and meta descriptions per route
- inspect internal links and destination status
- inspect image dimensions and delivery behavior
- verify local business information against the actual business data supplied by the owner
